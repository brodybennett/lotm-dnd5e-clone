# LoTM Sequence 1 Fairness Review

## Scope

This pass reviewed all current Sequence 1 abilities (`system.level = 8`) using the same internal power-budget lens as the earlier sequence reviews:

- overall combat and scene impact
- action economy efficiency
- spirituality cost versus payoff
- reliability and versatility
- scaling quality at higher spend
- synergy and abuse risk

The goal remained relative parity rather than role sameness, while preserving the expectation that Sequence 1 should feel like near-capstone authority: extremely dangerous, highly distinctive, and broadly scene-defining without letting a handful of no-cost baselines overshadow the rest of the tier.

## Overall Balance Observations

- Sequence 1 is the first tier where many pathways begin presenting proto-monarch authority. Conceptual edits, sovereign domains, hard execution tools, and broad support engines are common here.
- The tier is mostly healthy. Most pathways now have one very high-pressure authority effect, one strong utility or infrastructure tool, and one identity-defining high-ceiling package.
- The main overperformance risk remained zero-cost baseline abilities whose floor value already looked like a full angel-tier swing before any spend choice.
- The main underperformance risk came from support and inquiry abilities whose fantasy was correct but whose baseline payoff no longer kept up with the scale of the rest of the tier.

## Identified Outliers

### Overperforming

- `Death Decree` (Corpse Collector): the baseline `0 Spirituality` execution strike compressed too much damage, range, and collapse pressure into a no-cost floor.
- `Pale World` (Corpse Collector): the baseline `0 Spirituality` broad underworld weathering zone delivered too much recurring pressure and utility suppression for its size and duration.

### Underperforming

- `Arcana Bestowal` (Mystery Pryer): strong identity fit, but its baseline rider package was too light for Sequence 1 unless the scene heavily favored prepared utility over direct confrontation.
- `Emperor's Query` (Mystery Pryer): flavorful and useful, but its baseline payoff was still too information-heavy without enough immediate tactical conversion at this tier.

### Near-Edge But Left Unchanged

- `Dream of Reality` is extremely flexible, but it remains heavily GM-mediated and carries enough adjudication dependence that I left it unchanged.
- `Sovereign of Spirits` is very strong, but it is still bounded by soul-bearing targets, self-centered aura positioning, and charm-oriented counterplay.
- `Coincidence` and `Fate Forgery` are powerful zero-cost fate tools, but their contest reliance and plausibility/fate-interference constraints keep them inside the intended Monster lane for now.

## Applied Adjustments

### Death Decree

- Baseline cost increased from `0 Spirituality` to `1 Spirituality`.

Why:

- Prevents a very high-pressure execution tool from sitting too cheaply at the floor of Sequence 1.
- Preserves the decree fantasy and lethal threshold identity instead of forcing a broader damage rewrite.
- Keeps the multi-target, death-save, and instant-death riders as meaningful higher-spend authority.

### Pale World

- Baseline cost increased from `0 Spirituality` to `1 Spirituality`.

Why:

- Brings the recurring zone pressure and save-DC suppression package back into line with the rest of the tier.
- Preserves Corpse Collector's weathered underworld-domain identity rather than trimming away its signature pressure pattern.
- Keeps the large-radius, mobility suppression, and ongoing-effect decay riders as the real escalation points.

### Arcana Bestowal

- Baseline now also lets the target add `+1d4` once before the effect ends to one roll or check that directly uses the bestowed rider.

Why:

- Gives the ability a clearer immediate floor payoff.
- Preserves the pathway's analyzed-knowledge transfer identity.
- Improves competitiveness without turning the spell into a generic broad buff package.

### Emperor's Query

- Baseline now also lets you or one ally who acts on the extracted answer add `+1d6` to one related roll or save before the end of your next turn.

Why:

- Converts the information gained into clearer immediate tactical leverage.
- Preserves the query / extracted-truth fantasy instead of broadening it into hard control.
- Keeps the linked-question, multi-category, and lie-punishment riders as meaningful higher-spend upgrades.

## Remaining Concerns

- Sequence 1 is broadly healthy and needed another narrow outlier pass rather than a broad rebalance.
- Zero-cost baselines remain especially dangerous here because the tier's authority packages are large; they should keep being monitored closely.
- Some conceptual and prophecy tools remain highly GM-contextual by design. The goal here was to improve weak floors and trim obvious efficiency spikes, not erase that high-tier variability.
