# LoTM Sequence 4 Fairness Review

## Scope

This pass reviewed all current Sequence 4 abilities (`system.level = 5`) using the same internal power-budget lens as the earlier sequence reviews:

- overall combat and scene impact
- action economy efficiency
- spirituality cost versus payoff
- reliability and versatility
- scaling quality at higher spend
- synergy and abuse risk

The goal remained relative parity rather than role sameness, while preserving the expectation that Sequence 4 should feel like a saint-transition tier where pathways begin exerting strong specialist dominance without flattening their intended weaknesses.

## Overall Balance Observations

- Sequence 4 is the first tier where many pathways gain true scene-shaping authority. Large zones, durable transformations, remote influence, and broader tactical support are all meaningfully online here.
- The tier is mostly healthy. Most pathways now carry one strong encounter-defining tool, one specialist utility or tracking tool, and one high-ceiling expression of their core fantasy.
- The main overperformance risks were zero-cost baselines that created strong ongoing control or damage pressure without enough gating.
- The main underperformance risks were setup-heavy observation or sympathy abilities whose fantasy was correct but whose baseline scene contribution lagged behind neighboring Sequence 4 tools.

## Identified Outliers

### Overperforming

- `Night Domain` (Sleepless): the baseline ally-selective magical-darkness domain was too efficient at `0 Spirituality` for a full-minute area-control package.
- `Mental Plague` (Spectator): the baseline recursive damage-and-disruption effect applied too much ongoing pressure for a zero-cost single-save lock.

### Underperforming

- `Boundary Observatory` (Apprentice): strong identity fit, but its baseline payoff was too dependent on GM routing context and did not give enough immediate tactical return for a `2 Spirituality`, 1-minute setup.
- `Sympathetic Effigy` (Prisoner): the baseline effect was on-theme but too slow to matter in live play because it asked for an action setup before its control loop really started contributing.

### Near-Edge But Left Unchanged

- `Weapon Ointment Mastery` is strong, but it remains within Warrior's intended direct-combat lane and still depends on weapon access, profile relevance, and attack conversion.
- `Mind Dragon Breath` is aggressive for Spectator, but it is a one-shot burst rather than persistent damage dominance and stays bounded by save reliance and the pathway's otherwise non-martial kit.
- `Far Step Gate` is highly impactful mobility, but its baseline spirituality cost and destination restrictions keep it in line for Apprentice at this tier.

## Applied Adjustments

### Night Domain

- Baseline cost increased from `0 Spirituality` to `1 Spirituality`.

Why:

- Brings the domain's control floor back in line with other Sequence 4 battlefield authorities.
- Preserves Sleepless' darkness-domain identity rather than trimming away its core mechanics.
- Keeps the radius, stealth-support, and reaction-denial riders as meaningful escalation points.

### Mental Plague

- Baseline now allows the target to repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the plague on a success.

Why:

- Removes the most oppressive version of the zero-cost lock without changing the core fantasy of recursive intrusive-thought pressure.
- Better aligns Spectator with its intended weakness in sustained raw damage output.
- Preserves the stronger damage, splash, and reaction-denial upgrades as higher-spend improvements.

### Boundary Observatory

- Baseline now also lets you or one ally acting on an observed threshold add `+1d6` once during the duration to one Initiative, Stealth, Escape, or anti-ambush check before the outcome is confirmed.

Why:

- Gives the ability a clearer in-scene payoff instead of making it mostly pre-encounter route intelligence.
- Preserves the pathway's threshold-reading and route-control identity.
- Improves competitiveness without turning the ability into direct lockdown or teleportation.

### Sympathetic Effigy

- Baseline now lets you immediately trigger one effigy option when the link is formed without spending your bonus action.

Why:

- Fixes the slow setup problem while keeping the long-range curse-puppet identity intact.
- Improves action-economy efficiency without increasing raw control ceiling too sharply.
- Leaves the multi-target and free-trigger synergy riders as meaningful higher-spend upgrades.

## Remaining Concerns

- Sequence 4 is broadly healthy and needed only a light-touch pass.
- Several zero-cost baselines remain strong by design because this tier is supposed to feel like early authority play; the goal here was to trim only the clearest efficiency spikes.
- Information, route, and sympathy-based abilities still depend on campaign context more than direct combat packages. That is an intentional pathway distinction, not a balance flaw by itself.
