# LoTM Sequence 6 Fairness Review

## Scope

This pass reviewed all current Sequence 6 abilities (`system.level = 3`) using the same internal power-budget lens as the earlier sequence reviews:

- overall combat and scene impact
- action economy efficiency
- spirituality cost versus payoff
- versatility and reliability
- scaling quality at higher spend
- synergy and abuse risk

The target remained relative parity, not role homogenization.

## Overall Balance Observations

- Sequence 6 is the first tier where many pathways begin expressing durable authority rather than just strong tricks. The average floor is higher and more specialized than at Sequence 7.
- Most Sequence 6 packages are appropriately sharp: one strong scene/encounter tool plus one or two identity-defining support, ritual, or domain abilities.
- The clearest overperformers were durable self-buffs that generated too much passive floor value before any meaningful spend choice.
- The clearest underperformers were context-heavy analysis/divination abilities that still asked for significant GM/environment buy-in before paying back a Sequence 6 slot.

## Identified Outliers

### Overperforming

- `Dawn Armour` (Warrior): the baseline `+1 AC` plus `2 x Potency` temporary HP on a 10-minute bonus-action self-buff was too efficient for a no-spend Sequence 6 floor, especially with its later reaction mitigation and reform rider.

### Underperforming

- `Discerning Spiritual Materials` (Apothecary): strong identity fit, but baseline payoff was too dependent on crafting/material scenes and did not pull enough weight in live play when poison, disease, or curse-taint threats were present.
- `Knowledge of Spirit World` (Corpse Collector): flavorful and useful, but its baseline support die was a bit too low relative to the action, concentration, and scene-context requirements.
- `Temperament Trace` (Seer): thematically correct for impersonation and pursuit play, but baseline contribution was too thin unless the table heavily centered disguise and social infiltration.

### Near-Edge But Left Unchanged

- `Nullification` is very strong, but its high baseline spirituality cost, contest requirement, and built-in fallback weakening clause keep it within band.
- `Hypnosis` is powerful, but it is concentrated in single-target control with clear anti-mental and disruption counterplay.
- `Axiomatic Refutation` is efficient, but it is still a bounded defensive reaction gated by save failure and specific trigger conditions.

## Applied Adjustments

### Dawn Armour

- Baseline temporary hit points reduced from `2 x Potency` to `Potency`.

Why:

- Keeps Warrior durable without letting the baseline package dominate the Sequence 6 defense floor.
- Preserves the identity of conjured disciplined armor instead of reworking the ability.
- Leaves the higher-spend mitigation and reform options as the real escalation points.
- Works cleanly with the existing Sequence 5 legacy upgrade, which now restores the heavier shield feeling later in progression.

### Discerning Spiritual Materials

- Baseline now also allows `+1d4` on one saving throw against poison, disease, or curse-taint caused by one analyzed material.

Why:

- Gives Apothecary a more immediate scene-facing use case.
- Preserves the material-analysis identity rather than turning it into a generic combat tool.
- Improves floor value when the analyzed substances matter right now, not only during later crafting.

### Knowledge of Spirit World

- Baseline support die increased from `+1d4` to `+1d6`.
- The banked support die in its Sequence 5 legacy text was updated to match.

Why:

- Better matches the action and concentration commitment at Sequence 6.
- Keeps the ability firmly in the spirit-route / underworld-infrastructure lane.
- Raises competitiveness without changing scope or turning the ability into a hard-control tool.

### Temperament Trace

- Baseline now grants one `+1d4` during the trace window on a check to tail, expose, or intercept the traced profile, after seeing the roll but before the outcome is confirmed.

Why:

- Gives Seer a clearer immediate tactical payoff for the study time.
- Preserves the identity of behavioral reading, impersonation detection, and route-following.
- Improves floor value without inflating the stronger higher-spend pursuit guidance rider.

## Remaining Concerns

- Sequence 6 overall is fairly healthy and needed a lighter pass than the lower sequences.
- Warrior remains one of the best direct-combat paths at this tier, which is intended; this pass only trimmed the clearest baseline durability excess.
- Several analysis and spirit tools are still campaign-context sensitive by design. The goal here was to improve their floor, not erase those built-in weaknesses.
