# LoTM Sequence 0 Fairness Review

## Overall Balance Observations

Sequence 0 is broadly healthier than it first looks. The tier is intentionally authority-heavy, so raw cost parity is not the right lens by itself; several zero-cost baselines are acceptable because they are narrow, contested, identity-bound, or rely on concentration and specific targets. The main fairness issue was not "too much power exists at Sequence 0" but that a small number of free baselines were converting into too much immediate battlefield value relative to equally high-end paid capstones, while a couple of low-cost technical/support authorities still had floor-value problems when their preferred scene structure was absent.

The strongest performers at this tier were free baselines that combined strong range, reliable control or execution pressure, and little setup cost. The weakest performers were not numerically weak so much as too conditional, especially when they depended on preexisting scene objects or on long-form strategic play in a tier where many competitors produce immediate authority pressure.

## Identified Outliers

### Overperforming

- **Conceptual Extinction**: A free long-range execution tool with heavy damage, anti-healing, proxy suppression, and anti-revival pressure was over budget even by Sequence 0 standards.
- **Proxy of War**: A free bonus-action command package that simultaneously debuffed multiple enemies and granted repeated allied attack/save bonuses for a full minute was too efficient for Warrior's already reliable direct-combat profile.

### Underperforming

- **Digital Genesis Mandate**: Strong when valid symbolic infrastructure already existed, but too easy to low-roll into dead text when no qualifying construct was present.
- **Spirit World Mentor**: Strong in preparation, analysis, and extended scenes, but its baseline needed a more immediate high-tier payoff in active encounters and anti-control situations.

## Specific Adjustments Made

- **Conceptual Extinction**
  - Baseline cost increased from **0 Spirituality** to **1 Spirituality**.
  - Justification: kept the death-authority fantasy intact while adding a minimal cost gate to a capstone execution effect that was too efficient relative to other single-target Sequence 0 powers.

- **Proxy of War**
  - Baseline cost increased from **0 Spirituality** to **1 Spirituality**.
  - Justification: preserved Warrior's command-through-martial-pressure identity, but brought its action-economy efficiency closer to other top-tier teamfight authorities.

- **Digital Genesis Mandate**
  - Baseline now allows you to inscribe a temporary sigil scaffold on a visible unattended surface or object within range if no eligible construct is present.
  - Justification: raised reliability without altering the pathway's symbolic, technical, and revision-based fantasy.

- **Spirit World Mentor**
  - Baseline now lets one linked ally each round either add **+1d8** to one strategy, insight, knowledge-dependent check, or anti-confusion/charm/knowledge-injection save before rolling, or convert a failed check into a partial success with reduced effect.
  - Justification: improved immediate scene impact while staying aligned with Savant's mentorship, information-routing, and support-engineering identity.

## Remaining Concerns

- Corpse Collector and Spectator still sit near the top of the Sequence 0 pressure curve, but after this pass they read as "very strong within role" rather than clearly dominant across all roles.
- Several high-cost Bard and Reader capstones remain expensive, but their scene scale, authority breadth, and narrative leverage justify the premium more than the former free outliers did.
- If future playtesting shows too many zero-cost Sequence 0 baselines dominating repeated scene openings, the next place to watch is broad domain-style authorities rather than the narrower contested theft/revision tools.
