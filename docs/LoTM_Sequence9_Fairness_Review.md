# LoTM Sequence 9 Fairness Review

## Scope

This review compares all current Sequence 9 pathway abilities using the creation guide's shared power-budget mindset:

- overall mechanical and narrative impact
- action economy efficiency
- spirituality spend efficiency
- versatility and reliability
- scaling behavior at higher spend
- synergy and abuse risk

The goal was relative parity, not role homogenization.

## Overall Balance Observations

- Sequence 9 is broadly healthy in shape. Most pathways correctly deliver a two-ability starter kit with one clearer signature tool and one support, passive, or setup tool.
- The strongest Sequence 9 packages were the ones generating reliable combat value with little or no action cost, especially when paired with a second passive or long-duration self-buff.
- The weakest Sequence 9 packages were the ones whose payoff depended too heavily on outdoor, travel, weather, ritual, or highly specific table conditions.
- Information and narrative-utility pathways are generally within band when they also provide one practical in-scene edge. They start to slip when both abilities ask for ideal context before producing leverage.
- Support/control kits remain distinct and do not need to match martial kits on damage, but they still need a meaningful encounter-facing floor.

## Identified Outliers

### Overperforming

- `Criminal Proficiency` (Criminal): too much always-on combat efficiency for Sequence 9. Baseline passive `+Potency` damage on every turn, with no activation cost, outpaced comparable low-sequence striker packages like `Combat Mastery`, `Predator's Footwork`, and `Killzone Instinct`.

### Underperforming

- `Season Reading` (Planter): good identity fit, but too dependent on travel and natural-hazard scenes. In mixed campaigns it often contributed less than other Sequence 9 support abilities.
- `Detached Mind` (Spectator): reliable defense against emotional manipulation, but too narrow as a full Sequence 9 slot when compared with other pathways' broader passive/bonus-action posture tools.
- `Indexed Safeguard` (Reader): strong flavor and future ritual value, but baseline payoff skewed too far toward recall and downtime instead of immediate scene leverage.

### Near-Edge But Left Unchanged

- `Sunrise Chorus` is high-efficiency support, but its power is concentrated in overt team enabling and fear management, which fits Bard's intended early identity.
- `Contained Burst` is a strong defensive reaction package, but it remains tied to enemy hits, proximity, and reaction competition, which keeps it inside band.
- Marauder, Corpse Collector, and Seer remain somewhat table-dependent at Sequence 9, but not enough to justify direct changes in this pass.

## Applied Adjustments

### Criminal Proficiency

- Baseline bonus damage changed from `+Potency` to `+1`.
- `+2 Spirituality` rider changed from adding another `+Potency` to making the bonus damage become `+Potency` instead of `1`.

Why:

- Keeps Criminal as a straightforward early predator.
- Removes the clearest "free dominant striker" outlier at Sequence 9.
- Preserves the pathway's efficient violence identity without letting it outclass other combat starts before spending spirituality.

### Season Reading

- Baseline guidance die can now also apply to one initiative roll.
- `+2 Spirituality` weather-mobility rider now also covers movement penalties from nonmagical overgrowth.

Why:

- Raises the encounter-facing floor without changing the weather-reader fantasy.
- Makes the ability matter more often in ordinary scenes, not just travel or hazard-focused play.
- Keeps Planter distinct as a patient terrain-and-cycle support pathway instead of turning it into a generic combat buffer.

### Detached Mind

- Baseline now grants one `+1d4` bonus before the state ends to an Insight check or Spirituality save used to resist deception, intimidation, charm, or emotional provocation from a visible creature.

Why:

- Converts the ability from mostly passive insulation into a more practical contest tool.
- Better supports Spectator's "read and regulate minds correctly" identity.
- Improves reliability without changing the calm-detachment fantasy or making the ability offensively dominant.

### Indexed Safeguard

- Baseline now grants one `+1d4` bonus before the effect ends to an Arcana, History, or Investigation check, or to a save against charm, fear, or illusion, after seeing the roll but before resolution.

Why:

- Gives Reader a clearer immediate-use payoff at Sequence 9.
- Preserves the memory-index / analysis identity and ritual utility.
- Keeps the stronger reroll and ritual-efficiency riders on higher spend meaningfully better instead of replacing them.

## Remaining Concerns

- Planter still leans more campaign-context dependent than the median Sequence 9 pathway, but the package now has a better floor without forcing a larger rework.
- Criminal is still one of the more combat-forward starts, which is appropriate; it should simply no longer sit clearly above the other martial/predatory openers.
- Sequence 9 parity will likely need another pass after future chassis changes if HP/spirituality profiles and spirit-anchor stats are made pathway-specific.
