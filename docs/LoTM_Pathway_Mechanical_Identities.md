# LoTM Pathway Mechanical Identities

This document is a designer-facing shorthand reference for the pathways currently defined in `packs/lotm_pathways`.

It is meant to answer three questions quickly:

1. What is this pathway supposed to feel like in play?
2. What does it do better than other pathways?
3. What weaknesses should remain visible so the pathway keeps a distinct mechanical shape?

Weaknesses here are intentional design constraints, not flavor-only flaws. They are the counterweights that keep pathways differentiated during ability authoring, encounter design, and balance review.

## Apothecary
- Mechanical identity: A calm lunar support-controller that mixes diagnosis, healing, spirituality handling, transformation, and summoning into a methodical moon-alchemy toolkit.
- Strengths: Strong sustain, high utility, flexible ritual support, summon logistics, and good control over vitality or spirituality states.
- Weaknesses: Lower immediate lethality than pure offense pathways, wants preparation or resources to show full value, and can feel slow in short all-in fights.

## Apprentice
- Mechanical identity: A space-and-threshold skirmisher built around hidden routes, indirect access, evasive repositioning, and making ordinary positioning rules stop applying.
- Strengths: Exceptional mobility, infiltration, escape, bypass play, and strong battlefield geometry control.
- Weaknesses: Less direct kill pressure than dedicated war or curse pathways, depends heavily on route logic and spatial context, and loses impact if movement is hard-anchored or the fight becomes a pure damage race.

## Arbiter
- Mechanical identity: A declarative law controller that establishes jurisdiction, defines violations, and converts disorder into punishable openings.
- Strengths: Reliable crowd control, battlefield rule-setting, anti-chaos pressure, sentence chaining, and strong anti-anomaly utility.
- Weaknesses: Wants clear terms, territory, or declared rulings to reach full value, is less slippery than deception pathways, and can feel rigid when it cannot frame the conflict on lawful terms.

## Assassin
- Mechanical identity: A fast predatory calamity pathway that wins through allure, curses, afflictions, mobility, mirror tricks, and emotional destabilization.
- Strengths: High assassination pressure, strong debuff layering, excellent ambush tools, and dangerous snowball potential once it gains momentum.
- Weaknesses: Less durable in prolonged stand-up fights, many tools work best through proximity or psychological hooks, and it underperforms if denied tempo, concealment, or emotional leverage.

## Bard
- Mechanical identity: A radiant support-judgment pathway that turns morale, light, holiness, and contract authority into disciplined teamwide pressure.
- Strengths: Strong buffs, anti-fear coverage, purification, radiant punishment, and broad party stabilization.
- Weaknesses: Highly visible and overt, less subtle than shadow or dream pathways, and often spends early power budget on enabling others rather than ending fights alone.

## Corpse Collector
- Mechanical identity: A solemn death steward that treats corpses, spirits, and the Underworld as orderly infrastructure rather than spectacle.
- Strengths: Excellent spirit interaction, corpse utility, death-domain control, minion support, and strong late-game inevitability.
- Weaknesses: Often slower than frontloaded burst pathways, gains more value when death-state or spirit-state play is relevant, and is easier to pressure before its underworld machinery is established.

## Criminal
- Mechanical identity: A ruthless predator built around direct lethality, intimidation, weakness exploitation, and escalating degeneration into abyssal pressure.
- Strengths: High kill pressure, strong fear and corruption themes, straightforward threat projection, and brutal punishment of vulnerable targets.
- Weaknesses: Less subtle than social-control or information pathways, offers narrower team utility, and can become predictable if its intimidation and corrosion game is answered directly.

## Hunter
- Mechanical identity: An aggressive battlefield commander that combines pursuit, traps, target reading, formation pressure, and destructive war authority.
- Strengths: Excellent tempo setting, strong area denial, high pressure against exposed targets, and very good teamwide combat leadership.
- Weaknesses: Wants room, initiative, and battlefield structure to work with, is less useful in soft social scenes, and loses some edge when denied setup or forced into opaque information states.

## Lawyer
- Mechanical identity: A rhetoric-and-loophole controller that exploits wording, procedure, debt, and rule distortion to make hostile action collapse under its own logic.
- Strengths: Strong indirect control, debuff routing, leverage play, procedural punishment, and excellent scene-shaping through language.
- Weaknesses: Often needs context, leverage, or time to establish the best lines, is less honest in direct frontline exchanges, and can look underpowered if reduced to simple damage trading.

## Marauder
- Mechanical identity: A theft-and-error opportunist that steals resources, identity, function, or momentum before the opponent fully realizes what changed.
- Strengths: Excellent opportunistic play, strong disruption, high ceiling against powerful opponents, and flexible deception/infiltration value.
- Weaknesses: More reactive than brute-force pathways, depends on enemy assets being worth stealing or hijacking, and can feel thin if denied openings or forced into symmetrical combat.

## Monster
- Mechanical identity: A fate-and-probability pathway that wins through reversals, danger sense, misfortune routing, and controlled unreliability.
- Strengths: Hard to plan against, strong momentum flips, excellent omen play, and very high narrative pressure through luck distortion.
- Weaknesses: Intentionally non-deterministic, can create awkward team planning if pushed too far into chaos, and should never become perfectly reliable at producing exact outcomes.

## Mystery Pryer
- Mechanical identity: An occult scholar-diviner that turns questions, symbols, reenactment, and information revision into structured magical control.
- Strengths: Superb information gathering, ritual flexibility, foresight, remote knowledge pressure, and strong late-game reality-editing through information.
- Weaknesses: Wants interpretation and setup, carries corruption or knowledge-risk pressure, and is weaker in raw physical exchanges than martial or predatory pathways.

## Planter
- Mechanical identity: A patient life-and-cycle steward that uses growth, healing, poison, mutation, weather, and natural return as one continuous system.
- Strengths: Strong sustain, environmental shaping, organism control, gradual battlefield takeover, and broad life-domain versatility.
- Weaknesses: Usually slower than pure burst lines, prefers terrain, time, or living systems to interact with, and should not outpace dedicated controllers in instant hard lockdown.

## Prisoner
- Mechanical identity: A suppression pathway built around restraint, bottled violence, curses, monstrous release, and control through bindings, vessels, or objects.
- Strengths: Strong confinement tools, durable cursed forms, good curse architecture, and high payoff when it times its releases well.
- Weaknesses: Built-in tension and instability should stay real, bursts should feel timed rather than constant, and it is less elegant at broad support or clean ranged dominance.

## Reader
- Mechanical identity: An analysis-and-imitation pathway that studies systems, identifies flaws, and turns understanding into counters, replication, and concept-level edits.
- Strengths: Excellent problem solving, targeted counters, foresight, imitation of non-authority effects, and strong answer density once informed.
- Weaknesses: Needs information before it peaks, trends reactive instead of explosive, and should retain real limits when facing authority-grade protections or unknown systems.

## Sailor
- Mechanical identity: A storm-bringer that uses sea, lightning, wind, and sheer momentum to force movement, punish positioning, and overwhelm space.
- Strengths: Strong area pressure, movement manipulation, aggressive tempo, high spectacle damage, and good control over open battlefields.
- Weaknesses: Loud and conspicuous, less subtle in social or investigative play, and easiest to answer when fights deny room, line pressure, or momentum chaining.

## Savant
- Mechanical identity: An engineer-magus that solves problems through memory discipline, manufacturing, calibration, system control, and technical precision.
- Strengths: High preparation value, excellent utility, strong item and construct interaction, and very strong scaling through engineered solutions.
- Weaknesses: Can be preparation-heavy, loses some edge when deprived of tools or build time, and is less naturally dominant in emotional, legal, or faith-driven conflicts.

## Secrets Suppliant
- Mechanical identity: A secret-liturgy corrupter that blends whispered revelation, shadow devotion, bodily degeneration, and profane decree into dangerous sacramental power.
- Strengths: Strong shadow play, corruption pressure, body horror offense, exile/decree control, and excellent thematic overlap between divination and desecration.
- Weaknesses: Its power should feel risky and spiritually costly, it is less stable than cleaner law or support pathways, and its profile is usually too transgressive to stay low-friction for long.

## Seer
- Mechanical identity: A symbol-and-paradox trickster that moves from foresight and disguise into thread control, marionettes, historical projection, miracles, and Fool-style reality bending.
- Strengths: Exceptional misdirection, high survivability through substitution and escape tricks, strong information play, and enormous late-game flexibility.
- Weaknesses: Fragile if caught cleanly, relies on layered setup and sequencing, and should not outperform dedicated frontliners in direct exchanges without using its trick framework.

## Sleepless
- Mechanical identity: A nocturnal vigilance pathway that turns dream work, concealment, horror, spirits, and darkness into slow suffocating control.
- Strengths: Strong anti-ambush posture, dream manipulation, concealment, spirit command, and excellent attritional pressure through fear and night authority.
- Weaknesses: Slower to close fights than direct burst pathways, prefers psychological tempo over open force contests, and should lose some sharpness when dream/fear/concealment lines are resisted.

## Spectator
- Mechanical identity: A psychological observer-controller that dominates scenes through reading people correctly, regulating emotion, and reshaping minds or dreams rather than brute force.
- Strengths: Strong social control, telepathy, hypnosis, dream pressure, information extraction, and highly efficient scene manipulation.
- Weaknesses: Limited raw damage profile, reduced value against mindless or heavily shielded targets, and wants time or contact to convert insight into control.

## Warrior
- Mechanical identity: A stoic frontline combatant that combines martial mastery, survivability, anti-supernatural resistance, and twilight-inflected divine war pressure.
- Strengths: Excellent direct combat, high durability, strong anti-illusion and anti-corruption tools, and reliable value without elaborate setup.
- Weaknesses: Less subtle than knowledge or social pathways, narrower outside direct confrontation, and usually has to commit body and position to solve problems.

## Design Heuristics
- If a pathway's strengths start covering every major lane at once, its weaknesses are probably too soft.
- If a pathway can win cleanly outside its listed mechanical identity, it should usually pay with setup, risk, cost, or reduced reliability.
- When authoring new abilities, reinforce the pathway's core pressure pattern first and only then widen its utility footprint.
