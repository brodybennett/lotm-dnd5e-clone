# LoTM Sequence 7 Fairness Review

## Scope

This pass reviewed all current Sequence 7 abilities (`system.level = 2`) against the same internal power-budget standard used for the earlier sequence reviews:

- practical combat and scene impact
- action economy efficiency
- spirituality cost versus payoff
- reliability and versatility
- scaling quality at higher spend
- synergy and abuse risk

The goal remained relative parity, not uniform role output.

## Overall Balance Observations

- Sequence 7 generally feels like the point where pathway identities become decisively online. Most kits now have at least one ability with stable encounter leverage and one ability that widens the pathway's specialist lane.
- Compared with Sequence 8, there were fewer obvious weak records overall. Most differences at this tier were about reliability and floor value rather than missing impact.
- The main risk area was zero-cost or low-cost martial/self-buff baselines whose floor value stacked too efficiently for a full minute.
- The most at-risk underperformers were information/support abilities whose Sequence 7 fantasy was clear but whose baseline scene impact still lagged behind more direct combat-control packages.

## Identified Outliers

### Overperforming

- `Weapon Mastery` (Warrior): the baseline `+Potency` to attack rolls for 1 minute on a bonus action was too far above the accuracy budget of comparable Sequence 7 self-buffs. It compressed too much reliability into a zero-cost floor and threatened to outpace neighboring martial/control packages.

### Underperforming

- `Telepathy` (Spectator): good flavor and utility, but baseline payoff was too light for Sequence 7 when compared with other mind/control tools at the same tier.
- `Spirit Affinity` (Corpse Collector): well aligned to pathway identity, but too narrow and numerically thin at baseline outside heavily undead/spirit-focused scenes.

### Near-Edge But Left Unchanged

- `Crystal Route Reading` is niche, but its route/infiltration leverage remains appropriate for Apprentice and has meaningful higher-spend scaling.
- `Scene Reconstruction` is context-sensitive, but its concrete-output structure and reroll casefile rider keep it competitive in investigation-heavy play.
- `Placate` is a strong low-cost control tool, but it is tightly bounded by save reliance, direct-harm breakage, and the inability to fully suppress all hostile action patterns.

## Applied Adjustments

### Weapon Mastery

- Baseline attack bonus reduced from `+Potency` to `+1`.

Why:

- Keeps Warrior strong and reliable without letting one baseline buff dominate the Sequence 7 attack-accuracy band.
- Preserves the weapon-discipline fantasy and the sealed-weapon drawback mitigation.
- Leaves the damage and resistance-piercing upgrades as meaningful spend incentives instead of stacking on top of an already excessive floor.

### Telepathy

- Baseline now lets each participant add `+1d4` once during the link to one coordination check, Insight check, Perception check, or initiative roll tied to shared information from the link.

Why:

- Raises immediate scene value while staying fully on-theme for coordinated mind-link play.
- Keeps the ability from feeling like mostly flavor-plus-chat at Sequence 7.
- Preserves the stronger range, multi-link, and probing riders as higher-spend improvements instead of replacing them.

### Spirit Affinity

- Baseline save rider improved from `+1` to `+1d4` once per round on fear, charm, or possession-related saves forced by spirits or undead.
- `+1 Spirituality` text updated to refer to extending the baseline save rider to an ally.

Why:

- Improves the pathway's anti-spirit floor without changing its niche or fantasy.
- Makes the ability meaningfully relevant in the scenes where it is supposed to matter.
- Still keeps the power narrow enough that it does not become general-purpose universal defense.

## Remaining Concerns

- Sequence 7 is broadly healthier than Sequence 8 and needed a smaller touch pass.
- Warrior remains one of the strongest direct-combat pathways at this tier, which is intended; this pass only removed the clearest baseline accuracy outlier.
- Corpse Collector still leans context-sensitive in campaigns with little spirit/undead pressure, but that is a legitimate pathway weakness and should remain visible.
