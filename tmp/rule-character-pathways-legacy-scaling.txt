Legacy Ability Scaling
When designing a promotion package, preserve pathway continuity by explicitly investing in earlier sequence abilities so they remain relevant at higher tiers.
  - Reserve 25%-35% of gain budget for upgrading legacy abilities.
  - Upgrade up to 2 legacy abilities per promotion package.
  - Use one upgrade type per legacy upgrade: Potency (stronger numbers), Scope (range, targets, duration, or area), or Efficiency (frequency, setup/action cost, or resource pressure).
  - Score legacy upgrades with the same feature rubric and count their cost against sequence budget.
  - Relevance check: at least one ability from 2+ sequences below should remain tactically meaningful in normal play; if not, add a legacy upgrade or rider effect.
